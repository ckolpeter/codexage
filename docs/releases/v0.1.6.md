## Improved / 改進

- **Google sign-in**: Google refuses sign-in inside apps. As soon as the sign-in window reaches Google, Codexage now offers **Sign in with my browser**, which opens the paste window for that same account — instead of leaving you on Google's error page.
  **Google 登入**：Google 會封鎖 App 內的登入。現在登入視窗一跳到 Google，Codexage 就會提供 **「改用瀏覽器登入」**，直接打開同一個帳號的貼上視窗，不會再卡在 Google 的錯誤畫面。
- **Adding another account by pasting**: the paste window explains how — open a private window (Ctrl+Shift+N), sign in to the other account there, copy the session page, then just close the private window (don't click *Log out*).
  **用貼上方式新增帳號**：貼上視窗會說明做法：開啟無痕視窗（Ctrl+Shift+N），在那裡登入另一個帳號，複製 session 頁面後直接關掉無痕視窗（不要按「登出」）。
