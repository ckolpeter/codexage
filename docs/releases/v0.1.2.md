This release is about one moment: **when you hit a limit**, Codexage now tells you why, what's still usable, and what you can do about it. If you installed with the Windows installer, Codexage updates itself (restart from the tray menu when it says the update is ready).

這一版專注在一個時刻：**額度用完的時候**，Codexage 會告訴你原因、還有什麼能用，以及可以怎麼處理。用 Windows 安裝版的話會自動更新（系統匣選單出現提示時重新啟動即可）。

## New / 新功能

- **Why you're blocked.** At the top of the widget: your own limit, your workspace is out of credits, or you hit a cap set by your workspace, with advice that depends on whether you own the workspace. If it's your own limit and you have usage limit resets left, it reminds you that `/usage` in Codex CLI uses one.
  **用完的原因。** 小工具最上方會顯示：個人額度用完、工作區點數用完，或已達工作區設定的上限，並依你是不是工作區擁有者給出建議。如果是個人額度用完、而你還有額度重置次數，會提醒你在 Codex CLI 輸入 `/usage` 使用。
- **Monthly credit limit** (Business / Enterprise): "8,000 of 25,000 credits used" with a bar and reset countdown.
  **每月點數上限**（Business／Enterprise）：「已用 8,000 / 25,000 點」，附用量條與重置倒數。
- **Unavailable models**: which models can't be used right now and when they come back.
  **暫時無法使用的模型**：顯示哪些模型目前不能用，以及何時恢復。
- **Usage limit reset expiry**: the soonest expiry is shown under the count, and you get one reminder when a reset expires within a day, so it doesn't go to waste.
  **額度重置的到期時間**：次數下方顯示最近一次的到期時間；剩不到一天時提醒一次，避免浪費。
