## New / 新功能

- **Clipboard detection** in the paste window: after you copy `chatgpt.com/api/auth/session` (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C), the window shows "The clipboard has sign-in data for you@…" with a **Use it** button — no more clicking into the box and pasting. It checks when the window opens and every time you switch back to it. Pasting by hand still works.
  **貼上視窗會偵測剪貼簿**：複製 `chatgpt.com/api/auth/session`（Ctrl+A、Ctrl+C）後，視窗會顯示「剪貼簿裡有 you@… 的登入資料」，按 **「使用這份資料」** 即可，不用再點文字框貼上。視窗打開時、以及每次切回視窗時都會檢查。手動貼上的方式仍可使用。
- **Expired copy?** The window tells you right away, so you know to reload the page and copy again.
  **複製到過期的資料**時會馬上提示，讓你知道要重新整理網頁再複製。

## Privacy / 隱私

- Once the sign-in data is stored, **Codexage removes the token from the clipboard** (it works like a password). Anything else in your clipboard is never touched. The clipboard is read by the app itself; the token is never handed to the page.
  登入資料存好後，**Codexage 會把 token 從剪貼簿清除**（它等同你的密碼）。剪貼簿裡的其他內容都不會被動到。剪貼簿由程式本身讀取，token 不會交給視窗頁面。
